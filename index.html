Good to know

A few things you might be wondering.

Healthcare can be complicated. Getting to know Clover shouldn’t be.

What is Clover?

Clover is a health-care assistance app designed to help people feel more organized and supported as they navigate their health journey in Canada.

Does Clover replace my doctor or health-care provider?

No. Clover is intended to support your health-care journey, not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always speak with a qualified health-care professional about your care.

Is Clover for emergencies?

No. Clover is not an emergency service and is not intended for urgent medical needs. If you may be experiencing an emergency, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department.

Where can I use Clover?

Clover is being designed with Canada’s health-care landscape in mind. App availability and services may vary; check the app for current availability in your area.
